1Arm and planar guard
The guard is the only writer to MoveIt Servo: it locks the tool vertical at push height (– m) and keeps the tip inside the push area. Drive it only with the remapped keyboard_teleop below (plain keyboard_teleop would bypass the guard). If the guard reports eef_outside_area, disable it and move the tool over the sheet first (e.g. with the MoveIt moves on the robot control page).
keyboard_teleop–
Run this in a terminal and keep it focused. Press p (POSE), keep the base frame (w), then drive with the arrow keys. Its pose target goes to /pusht/teleop_target, so the guard's limits apply; its twist, joint and mode-switch outputs are remapped to unused topics, so t, j, e do nothing, and the warning switch_command_type not available is expected. The z keys are ignored (height is locked).

2Camera ROI
The ROI is cut from the camera frame and resized to 256 × 256. Auto ROI uses exactly the push area (the guarded area, orange) from the camera TF, or type the pixel values and press Apply ROI. Keep it fixed across a dataset: the policy learns this exact view.
3Record episodes
Teleop: recording runs until Stop & save or the episode length. Policy: always the full episode length (Stop is ignored; Discard or Abort end it). Move the T anywhere you like in Isaac Sim between episodes; with wait for Record you can drive with keyboard_teleop until you press Record (press p in keyboard_teleop after the arm went home, see step 1).
