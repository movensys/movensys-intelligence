1Arm and planar guard
The guard is the only writer to MoveIt Servo: it locks the tool vertical at push height (– m) and keeps the tip inside the push area. Do not run keyboard_teleop at the same time. If the guard reports eef_outside_area, disable it and press Park first.

2Camera ROI and T colour
The ROI is cut from the camera frame and resized to 256 × 256. Auto fits the push area from the camera TF; or use Draw ROI on the image. Keep it fixed across a dataset: the policy learns this exact view.
3Record episodes
Teleop: recording runs until Stop & save or the episode length. Policy: always the full episode length (Stop is ignored; Discard or Abort end it). Move the T anywhere you like in Isaac Sim between episodes; with wait for Record the arm holds (and you can drive it) until you press Record.
